# AI assistance and responsibility

AI coding tools can support inspection, drafting, and review, but the resulting text or code still needs verification. This portfolio distinguishes documented assistance from assumptions about how earlier research was produced.

## Supported acknowledgment

The project conversation directly records Codex assisting with local file inspection, identifying publication-sensitive material, drafting English portfolio documentation, and preparing a local release review. These activities support the acknowledgment in the README.

This edition does not claim that Codex authored particular original model functions, performed particular historical training runs, or independently validated the research. It does not infer tool use from a file's existence or from another AI-written description.

## Human contributions

The project owner specified the purpose, chronological evaluation constraints, acceptance conditions, preservation rules, and publication boundaries. The owner also required exclusion of material with uncertain publication rights and retained final publication approval.

This division of responsibility does not imply sole authorship of the underlying research implementation. A fuller authorship statement would require a separate provenance review before publication.

## Limits of the acknowledgment

No development-time savings, performance improvement attributable to AI assistance, competition award, community adoption, or production deployment is claimed. The project is independent and does not claim sponsorship, endorsement, or ambassador selection by OpenAI.

The documentation can be read without paid services. That statement does not assert that every tool used throughout the project's history was free.
